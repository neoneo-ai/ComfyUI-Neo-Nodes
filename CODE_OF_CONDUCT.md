# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

> 🇨🇳 中文：[CODE_OF_CONDUCT_ZH.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT_ZH.md)

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy
community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and for the users who run this plugin.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information, including their API keys, model paths, or machine layout.
- Publishing a provider key, a user's gallery, or a user's config in an issue, a PR, or a screenshot.
- Publishing, shipping, or pushing code that phones home from a user's machine without an explicit user
  action behind it.

## Enforcement

Report enforcement matters to the maintainers privately through GitHub private vulnerability reporting on
[neoneo-ai/neo-studio](https://github.com/neoneo-ai/neo-studio) (Security → Report a vulnerability), or by
opening an issue when the report is not sensitive. Reports are reviewed by the organization owners; the
maintainer team decides on warnings, temporary bans, permanent removal, and public correction, and publishes
the outcome when the reporter agrees it can be public.

Maintainers are held to the same standard. A maintainer who is named in a report stops handling the code that
report touches until it is resolved.
